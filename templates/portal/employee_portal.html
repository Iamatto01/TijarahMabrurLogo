How to Configure Employee Portal (Google Sheets / Airtable / Notion)

The Employee Portal allows your internal team to view real-time operations schedules, task lists, and work assignments directly inside your Client Portal via an embedded spreadsheet or document.

Option 1: Set Environment Variable (Recommended for Production / Render)

  1. Open your Google Sheet / Airtable / Notion page.
  2. Copy the page / share link or embed link.
  3. Set the environment variable on your server (e.g. Render Dashboard environment settings):
    EMPLOYEE_SHEET_URL=https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/.../edit
